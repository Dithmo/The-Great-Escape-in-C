Options

Keys

WASD, Enter and 1/2 always work as alternatives. Esc or P pauses. C toggles the classic view, M the minimap, F5/F9 quick save and load.

How to play

You are a prisoner of war. Follow the camp routine (roll call, breakfast, exercise, bed) to keep the guards calm, and sneak off to gather what you need to escape. Your morale flag drops when you get caught, miss roll call or lose items; if morale hits zero you give up.

Movement is isometric: Up walks towards the top-left, Right top-right, Down bottom-right, Left bottom-left. If you stop giving input the hero follows the routine on his own (autopilot) and guards lose interest in him.

E pick up (fire + up)   Q drop (fire + down)   Z use item 1 (fire + left)   X use item 2 (fire + right). You can carry two items.

Keys open their doors, the lockpick opens any lock (takes a while), wire cutters cut through fences, the shovel clears the collapsed tunnel, the torch lights tunnels, poison on food distracts dogs, a bribe turns a prisoner into a decoy. Leave the camp with the right items to escape for good: papers, compass, purse and the uniform all matter.

A red flag means you are somewhere you should not be. Being caught means solitary confinement and losing your items.
